# Chest Pain and Breathing Difficulty - Emergency Guidance
**Important:** Chest pain, pressure, or sudden/severe shortness of breath can be signs of a medical emergency.
**Seek emergency care immediately for:** chest pain/pressure/tightness, pain spreading to arm/jaw/back, sudden shortness of breath, fainting, severe dizziness, confusion, bluish lips, coughing up blood, or signs of stroke.
**Do not self-diagnose:** Causes range from non-urgent to life-threatening. Prompt medical evaluation is important.
**Source:** American Heart Association - Warning Signs of a Heart Attack; CDC - Heart Attack Symptoms; MedlinePlus - Chest pain.
