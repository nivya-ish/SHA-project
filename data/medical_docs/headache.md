# Headache - General Information
**Overview:** Headaches have many causes, including tension, migraine, dehydration, infections, and other medical conditions. Most headaches are not emergencies, but some require urgent evaluation.
**When to seek care:** Consult a clinician for frequent, severe, worsening, or new-pattern headaches, or headaches with neurological symptoms.
**Emergency warning signs:** Seek emergency care for sudden "worst headache of life," headache after head injury, confusion, fainting, seizure, weakness/numbness, trouble speaking, vision loss, stiff neck with fever, or persistent vomiting.
**Source:** Mayo Clinic - Headache; MedlinePlus - Headache.
