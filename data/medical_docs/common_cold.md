# Common Cold
**Overview:** The common cold is a mild upper respiratory infection, often caused by viruses such as rhinoviruses. Symptoms may include runny or stuffy nose, sore throat, cough, sneezing, headache, and mild fatigue.
**Typical course:** Symptoms often improve within about 7-10 days, though cough may last longer in some people.
**Self-care:** Rest, fluids, and over-the-counter symptom relief may help some people when used appropriately. Follow product labels and ask a pharmacist or clinician if unsure.
**When to seek care:** Seek medical advice if symptoms are severe, worsen, do not improve, or if you have trouble breathing, chest pain, high fever, dehydration, or underlying health conditions.
**Source:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) - Common Cold; MedlinePlus - Common Cold.
