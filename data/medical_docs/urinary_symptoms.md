# Urinary Symptoms
**Overview:** Burning urination, frequent urination, urgency, cloudy/foul-smelling urine, or pelvic discomfort can occur with urinary tract infections and other conditions.
**When to seek care:** Contact a healthcare professional for urinary symptoms, especially if persistent, recurrent, severe, or associated with fever, back/flank pain, nausea/vomiting, pregnancy, or blood in urine.
**Urgent care:** High fever, severe flank pain, confusion, or inability to keep fluids down may need prompt evaluation.
**Source:** CDC - Urinary Tract Infection; MedlinePlus - Urinary tract infections.
