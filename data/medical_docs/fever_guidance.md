# Fever in Adults - General Guidance
**Overview:** Fever is a temporary rise in body temperature and is often a sign that the body is responding to infection or inflammation. Associated symptoms depend on the cause.
**Self-care:** Rest, fluids, and light clothing may help comfort. Use fever-reducing medicines only as directed and when appropriate for you.
**When to seek medical care:** Seek medical advice for persistent fever, very high temperature, severe symptoms, dehydration, confusion, stiff neck, rash, breathing difficulty, or if you are immunocompromised, pregnant, or have serious chronic illness.
**Emergency:** Seek urgent care for seizures, severe headache with confusion, trouble breathing, chest pain, or signs of sepsis.
**Source:** Mayo Clinic - Fever; MedlinePlus - Fever.
