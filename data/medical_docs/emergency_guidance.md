# When to Seek Emergency Medical Care
**Call emergency services or seek immediate care for:**
- Trouble breathing or severe shortness of breath
- Chest pain or pressure
- Signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty, sudden confusion)
- Severe bleeding
- Seizures or loss of consciousness
- Severe allergic reaction (swelling of face/lips/tongue, difficulty breathing, widespread hives)
- Sudden severe headache, severe abdominal pain, or rapidly worsening symptoms
- New confusion, blue/gray lips or face, or signs of shock
**Note:** This list is not complete. If you are concerned that symptoms may be life-threatening, seek emergency help.
**Source:** MedlinePlus - When to use the emergency room; CDC emergency warning guidance; WHO emergency care principles.
