# Gastroenteritis and Dehydration
**Overview:** Gastroenteritis often causes diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, stomach cramps, and sometimes fever. Viral infections are common causes.
**Self-care:** Small frequent sips of fluids or oral rehydration solutions can help prevent dehydration. Rest and gradual return to eating may help as tolerated.
**When to seek care:** Seek medical advice for signs of dehydration (very dry mouth, dizziness, little or no urination), blood in stool or vomit, severe abdominal pain, high fever, or symptoms lasting more than a few days.
**Urgent care:** Infants, older adults, pregnant people, and those with weakened immune systems may need earlier assessment.
**Source:** CDC - Viral Gastroenteritis; NHS - Diarrhoea and vomiting.
