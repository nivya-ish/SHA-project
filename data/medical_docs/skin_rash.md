# Skin Rash and Itching
**Overview:** Skin rashes and itching can be caused by allergies, infections, irritants, eczema, fungal infections, insect bites, or other conditions. Appearance, distribution, and associated symptoms help determine the cause.
**When to seek care:** Seek medical advice for widespread or painful rash, rash with fever, signs of infection (pus, warmth, swelling), rapidly spreading rash, or rash that does not improve.
**Emergency:** Seek urgent care for rash with trouble breathing, swelling of face/lips/tongue, fainting, or signs of anaphylaxis.
**Source:** MedlinePlus - Rashes; American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) - Rash information; NHS - Rashes.
