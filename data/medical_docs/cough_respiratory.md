# Cough and Respiratory Symptoms
**Overview:** Cough can occur with infections, allergies, asthma, acid reflux, or other conditions. Associated symptoms such as fever, wheeze, chest pain, or shortness of breath help guide evaluation.
**When to seek care:** Contact a healthcare professional if cough lasts more than a few weeks, is worsening, or is accompanied by coughing up blood, weight loss, night sweats, or persistent fever.
**Urgent symptoms:** Seek immediate care for severe shortness of breath, bluish lips/face, chest pain, confusion, or inability to speak in full sentences.
**Source:** MedlinePlus - Cough; National Health Service (NHS) - Cough.
